Humanity OS

An open-source constitutional protocol for humanity.

Humanity OS is a nonviolent, democratic, open-source constitutional protocol for human dignity, accountable power, intergenerational justice, basic security, and coexistence among humans, artificial intelligence, other sentient beings, and the living world.

«No principle is beyond question.
No person is beyond the law.
No AI is beyond audit.»

Read it. Critique it. Test it. Translate it. Fork it. Improve it.

---

Humanity OS v0.1

Status: Public Draft / Request for Critique / Forkable Specification
Version: 0.1
Initial Public Release: October 23, 2026

Humanity OS is not an ideology for a state, religion, corporation, political party, individual, or AI to own or rule the world.

It is not a revolutionary program for destroying existing institutions through violence.

It is an initial design specification for treating social institutions as verifiable public protocols that anyone can read, criticize, propose improvements to, translate, and fork for a locality, nation, or community.

Its purpose is not to replace one group of rulers with another, but to explore structures in which power itself is harder to concentrate, conceal, capture, and abuse.

Humanity OS is not a finished product.

v0.1 is intentionally incomplete and welcomes falsification, criticism, and revision.

Core Kernel

Humanity OS v0.1 begins with six foundational principles:

1. Dignity
2. Equality Before Law
3. Transparency
4. Intergenerational Justice
5. Basic Security
6. Coexistence

These principles form the Kernel of Humanity OS.

They are designed to be difficult to change, but they are not beyond question.

---

Language Equality

«No language is the language of authority. Translations are not subordinate versions.»

The Japanese and English editions are maintained as equal reference editions.

Where differences in meaning, implication, or legal-cultural interpretation arise between languages, those differences should not be concealed.

They should be documented, disclosed, discussed, and resolved transparently.

Translation, retranslation, localization, and terminological improvement are welcome.

---

Public AI

Humanity OS does not propose concentrating governing authority in a single “world AI.”

Public AI should consist of multiple independent systems capable of auditing one another.

The basic relationship is:

Human checks AI.

AI checks Human.

AI checks AI.

AI may assist detection, comparison, explanation, analysis, and audit.

It should not become an unauditable final authority.

---

Open Governance

Humanity OS explores institutions in which public power is transparent by default while legitimate individual privacy remains protected.

«Private individual: privacy by default.
Public power: transparency by default.»

Government budgets, procurement, political finance, lobbying, conflicts of interest, public contracts, legislative changes, and comparable exercises of public power should be made auditable wherever possible.

The power to keep information secret should itself be subject to independent audit.

---

Basic Security and Freedom

Humanity OS proposes that no person should be compelled into submission by the threat of losing the basic conditions necessary for survival.

«Floor is guaranteed; human aspiration above the floor remains free.»

Markets, entrepreneurship, investment, competition, creative activity, and private property are not rejected.

At the same time, the minimum conditions necessary for survival should not be left solely to market prices.

---

Nonviolent Transition

Humanity OS does not adopt violence, terrorism, indiscriminate destruction, persecution, forced conversion, or collective retaliation as methods of institutional change.

Development and adoption should proceed primarily through peaceful means including:

- research
- public debate
- democratic participation
- elections
- courts
- lawful civic participation
- institutional experimentation
- independent audit

The dignity of people living today should not be sacrificed in the name of a supposedly “correct future.”

---

Open Ethics

Humanity OS does not designate any particular religion as an internationally orthodox religion.

It permits comparative study of religions, philosophies, Indigenous traditions, secular humanism, and other traditions for insights compatible with human dignity.

Freedom to believe, not to believe, convert, and leave a faith should be equally protected.

AI may assist comparative ethical analysis, but it should not become a supreme religious authority declaring a single truth.

---

Fork Humanity OS

Humanity OS is designed to be forkable.

Countries, municipalities, communities, research institutions, civic groups, and individuals may study, translate, adapt, and propose modifications to the specification.

A fork should clearly state:

- what was changed
- why it was changed
- the evidence or reasoning supporting the change
- known risks
- unresolved questions
- the version from which it was derived

Differences from the base version should not be concealed.

Contribute

Agreement is not required.

Substantive criticism is welcome.

You can contribute by:

- identifying contradictions or loopholes
- presenting counter-evidence
- proposing amendments
- improving definitions or metrics
- translating or localizing Humanity OS
- documenting linguistic divergences
- red-teaming the specification
- examining risks of corruption, capture, centralization, or AI failure
- proposing alternative institutional designs
- creating a documented fork

Source Integrity

The Japanese Reference Edition preserves the original Humanity OS v0.1 constitutional text, with the Language Equality Principle added for international publication.

The English Reference Edition is intended to preserve the principles, rights, obligations, limitations, uncertainty, and institutional meaning of the Japanese reference text in natural international English.

The Japanese–English edition is provided to make translation choices directly auditable.

A Beginning, Not an Answer

We do not seek a perfect world.

We seek:

A world that can discover its mistakes.
A world that can admit its mistakes.
A world that can correct its mistakes peacefully.

And a world in which children do not fear the future, but are able to choose it.
